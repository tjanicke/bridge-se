Concrete Box Culvert Check

Reinforced concrete box culverts, one to four cells. Cast-in-place boxes by the IDOT Culvert Manual (2017), pinned or rigid-frame, with live load by the Culvert Manual or the AASHTO LRFD 10th Edition; proposes the lightest design and compares single cells with the IDOT standard tables. Precast box sections by the ASTM C1577 Appendix X1 design criteria: required reinforcement areas As1 to As8 and shear. Every box carries its HL-93 design load rating by the MBE (6A.5.12, LRFR).
