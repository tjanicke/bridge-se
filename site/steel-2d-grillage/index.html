How the framing is built

  • Coordinates. Feet and degrees. In plan, x is east and y is north. Offsets are positive to the right looking up-station. A skew turns the support line counterclockwise from the line normal (radial) to the alignment.
  • Girders. Each girder is a line in plan: a constant or straight-line-varying offset from the alignment (so curved, flared and tapered girders are all the same input), or a list of x-y points. A girder runs between its end supports, extended past the bearings by the distance entered, or it can start or end on a diaphragm or another support line. Spans are measured along each girder between its bearings and framing ends.
  • Cross-frames. With square bearings, each span is divided equally, so on a curve the lines are radial and line up across the bays. Where a bay has a skewed bearing, cross-frames are normal to the girders and are laid out bay by bay over the length where a normal line lands inside a span of the next girder, so they stagger near skewed supports (AASHTO 6.7.4.2: skews over 20°). With a skew of 20° or less they can be set parallel to the skew instead. Cross-frames along the support lines are added between girders that bear on the same support. Any cross-frame can be removed by clicking it, and cross-frames can be added by girder and distance.
  • DXF import. Lines, polylines (with arcs) and arcs are read by layer. You assign each layer a role: girders, support lines, diaphragms or cross-frames. Girder pieces that meet end to end are joined; cross-frame lines are attached to the girders their ends touch. The drawing units come from the file and can be changed. The import becomes an X-Y input you can edit.
  • Next stages. Section properties along each girder and the grillage stiffness model (girder warping torsion, cross-frames as condensed trusses with the reduced axial stiffness of single angles, composite deck), dead-load stages and fit conditions, live load by influence surface, cross-frame member forces, and the hand-off of girder forces to the AASHTO checks already used in Steel Line Girder.
