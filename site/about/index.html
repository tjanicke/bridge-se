Bridge structural engineering

About Bridge SE

Bridge SE is a free, open-source collection of bridge structural engineering check tools. It is part of Open DOT.

What it is

Each module is a focused calculation tool for a common bridge design or rating task. Each one shows its method, the code articles it follows and its assumptions, so you can check the work instead of trusting a black box.

  • Just Live LoadContinuous-beam live-load envelopes: HL-93, HS loading, AASHTO routine commercial trucks and permits.
  • Steel Line GirderContinuous steel girder analysis, live-load envelopes and selected AASHTO LRFD checks.
  • Concrete Line GirderIDOT precast prestressed I, bulb-T, IL and deck beams: strength, service, shear and load rating.
  • Concrete Slab BridgeContinuous concrete slab bridges: equivalent-strip analysis, design checks and HL-93 load rating.
  • Concrete Box CulvertCast-in-place and precast box culverts, single and multi-cell, with HL-93 load rating.
  • Just Lap SplicesReinforcing-bar development and lap splice lengths across AASHTO LRFD editions.

Your data stays in your browser

Every calculation runs in your web browser. Inputs are saved only in your own browser’s local storage, so a model is there when you come back on the same device. Nothing you enter, and no result, is sent to a server.

The site uses basic, cookie-free web analytics to count page visits and which tool features are used, such as running a check or printing a report. It never collects your inputs or results.

Open source

The complete source code for every module is public on GitHub at github.com/tjanicke/bridge-se, under the MIT License. You may use, study, change and share it, including for commercial work, provided the copyright and license notice are kept with it.

Engineers can read exactly how a result is calculated. If you find an error or have a suggestion, open an issue on GitHub or email admin@open-dot.org.

How the modules are checked

Modules are developed with the help of AI coding assistants. Before a module or an update is published, it is checked against hand calculations, published design examples and independent solvers, and reviewed in an independent audit. Each module’s method notes list its assumptions, limits and the code articles it follows.

Engineering use and limits

Bridge SE tools are aids for checking, study and preliminary work. They are not a substitute for professional engineering judgment, and they do not seal, certify or approve a design or rating.

  • Verify every result independently before using it for design, rating or construction.
  • You are responsible for confirming that a tool, its assumptions and its code edition apply to your structure and to your agency’s requirements.
  • The software is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind, as stated in the MIT License.

Codes and independence

The modules cite AASHTO, ASTM and Illinois DOT publications by article number. They do not reproduce copyrighted specification text; you will need your own copies of the governing codes.

Bridge SE is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by AASHTO, ASTM, FHWA, IDOT or any other agency or employer. All tools are free to use.
